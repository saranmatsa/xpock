# XPOCK Research Results

## Topic
**Can information be transmitted through a single finite physical wave whose physical properties, structure, or interactions enable a receiver to reconstruct previously unknown information?**

## Methodology
- 1020 distinct research queries across 34 themes, searched in batches of 30 via `web_search`.
- Themes: classical waves, quantum waves, unconventional physical properties, information representation, physical interactions, reconstruction, existing research, unexplored combinations.
- Start from first principles; no assumption of a particular wave, mechanism, or architecture.
- Constraint: receiver must not already possess the information; not retrieval/selection/unlocking of pre-existing data.
- Laptop-only resources; no specialized hardware or paid infrastructure.
- Do not assume new physics is necessary; do not dismiss unconventional possibilities without investigation.
- Do not fabricate sources, research, or results.

## Evidence Classification
- **ESTABLISHED** — peer-reviewed / textbook consensus, reproducible
- **HYPOTHESIS** — proposed but not conclusively demonstrated
- **SPECULATION** — conjectural, no experimental basis

## Batch 1 Results — Q001-Q030: Classical Wave Information Theory Fundamentals

### Q001: Maximum information capacity of a classical electromagnetic wave Shannon limit
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Shannon channel capacity C = W log2(1+SNR) bits/s for AWGN channel. Electromagnetic channel capacity bounds derived via Holevo information for thermal channels; applies to any bosonic thermal-noise channel including EM signalling at any frequency.
**Source:** MIT Giovannetti et al. Nature Photon 7, 834–838 (2013); https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/129b4ecb-c199-40e6-a63b-b69c450f5be1

### Q002: Wave information theory physical wave encoding capacity limits
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. "Wave Theory of Information" (Cambridge UP) derives information-theoretic laws relating functional approximation, quantum uncertainty, entropy, capacity, rate distortion, and degrees of freedom of band-limited radiation using EM waves.
**Source:** Cambridge University Press book page; https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/wave-theory-of-information/8F3C47FFABA1A7F274026C812D117EA4

### Q003: Can a single wave pulse carry arbitrary information classical wave
**Result:** ESTABLISHED (with caveat). A pulse = single disturbance carrying information in its shape; truly monochromatic infinite carrier carries no information itself (only modulation does). No physical object moves but energy and information cross.
**Source:** OpenStax Univ Physics Vol 1 Ch 16; Strassler "Waves (Classical Form)"; https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/16-2-mathematics-of-waves

### Q004: Classical wave modulation information encoding amplitude frequency phase
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Three primary modulation parameters: amplitude (AM), frequency (FM), phase (PM). x(t) = A(t)cos(2πfct + Θ(t)). AM: info in amplitude; FM: info in instantaneous frequency; PM: info in phase. QAM combines AM+PM.
**Source:** UT Austin modulation handout; MIT OCW 6.003 Lecture 24; Wellesley PDF; https://users.ece.utexas.edu/~bevans/courses/realtime/handouts/modulation/modulation.pdf

### Q005: Information content of a single finite wave packet
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Wave-packet = localized superposition of plane waves; shorter pulse requires larger frequency bandwidth (classical uncertainty principle). Plane waves carry no information; pulses do.
**Source:** Physics LibreTexts 2.10 Wave-Packets; https://phys.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Quantum_Mechanics/Introductory_Quantum_Mechanics_(Fitzpatrick)/02:_Wave-Particle_Duality/2.10:_Wave-Packets

### Q006: Wave equation information propagation finite speed
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Hyperbolic wave equations have finite propagation speed; domain of dependence limited by characteristic cone. Value at point depends only on initial data within domain of dependence.
**Source:** arXiv 1106.0817; Cambridge JWL 1977; Stanford Math 220A handout; https://arxiv.org/pdf/1106.0817

### Q007: Classical wave superposition information encoding multiple signals
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Superposition principle: when two or more waves overlap, resulting disturbance = algebraic sum of individual disturbances. Infinite number of waves can occupy same space without affecting one another.
**Source:** Britannica Principle of Superposition; https://www.britannica.com/science/principle-of-superposition-wave-motion

### Q008: Wave interference patterns information storage reconstruction
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Random interference wavefront can reconstruct high-resolution ultrasound images via joint image reconstruction from single pulse-echo transmission. Phase-only reconstruction possible from Fourier transform phase.
**Source:** PMC7698025 ultrasound imaging; APS 2025 phase-only reconstruction; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7698025

### Q009: Can information be encoded in wave shape alone classical
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Wave shape (waveform f(t)) carries information; normal modes superposition = "the pluck"; wavepacket motion/dephasing/rephasing encodes information in the shape.
**Source:** MIT 5.61F17 Lecture 4 Classical Wave Equation; https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/5-61-physical-chemistry-fall-2017/

### Q010: Physical wave as information carrier theoretical limits
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Ideal carrier wave (single frequency fixed amplitude) carries only frequency/amplitude/phase — not useful message information. Practical waves have natural broadness giving degrees of freedom; all practical waves can convey information via modulation.
**Source:** Physics Stack Exchange carrier wave info; arXiv 1406.4968; https://physics.stackexchange.com/questions/798609/why-carrier-wave-cannot-transfer-information

### Q011: Classical field theory information encoding in wave modes
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Classical wave equation separation of variables → normal modes; superposition of modes = "the pluck"; wavepacket motion/dephasing/rephasing.
**Source:** MIT 5.61F17 Lecture 4; https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/5-61-physical-chemistry-fall-2017/

### Q012: Wave bandwidth information capacity relationship classical waves
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Wave Theory of Information derives capacity limits related to degrees of freedom of band-limited radiation; capacity bounded by bandwidth and SNR.
**Source:** Cambridge Wave Theory of Information book; https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/wave-theory-of-information/8F3C47FFABA1A7F274026C812D117EA4

### Q013: Orthogonal wave modes information multiplexing classical waves
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. OAM modes with different azimuthal/radial indices are orthogonal; OAM multiplexing increases capacity. Demonstrated THz OAM at 0.3 THz: 60 Gbit/s QPSK, 24 Gbit/s 16-QAM; 80 Gbit/s QPSK via 2 pol × 2 freq × 2 OAM.
**Source:** IEEE OMDM; IEEE HODM; PubMed 36237073; https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8581019

### Q014: Wavefront shaping information encoding classical optics
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Wavefront shaping enables control of classical light through scattering media; fundamental bounds on restoring correlations. Non-orthogonal polarization encoding/decoding assisted by structured optical pattern recognition.
**Source:** AIP 2025 fundamental bounds; Optica OE-30-23-42026; https://pubs.aip.org/aip/app/article/10/11/110801/3370522/

### Q015: Spatial light modulation wave information encoding
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Technique to encode both amplitude and phase information onto a single binary phase-only spatial light modulator via spatial modulation of diffraction efficiency.
**Source:** PubMed 12699347 Davis et al. Applied Optics 42(11); https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12699347

### Q016: Temporal wave shaping information encoding pulses
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Spectral broadening and shaping of nanosecond pulses toward single-photon shaping; nonlinear wave interactions enable spatio-temporal pulse shaping; THz pulse shaping via photoexcited semiconductor.
**Source:** NIST pub 916179; PMC4933904; AIP APL 106 051110; https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=916179

### Q017: Classical wave coherence information preservation
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Linear and angular coherence momenta introduced into general coherence theory of vector EM fields; coherence conservation laws as continuity equations for propagation of second-order correlation tensors.
**Source:** PubMed 16902605; Springer Classical and Quantum Interference and Coherence; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16902605

### Q018: Decoherence classical waves information loss mechanism
**Result:** ESTABLISHED (contested interpretation). Decoherence = environment-induced superselection; traditionally information loss to environment. PRL 2025 challenges this: decoherence implies information gain, not loss.
**Source:** arXiv quant-ph/0306072; Physics Today Zurek 1991; PRL PhysRevResearch.7.L012040; https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0306072

### Q019: Wave scattering information recovery inverse scattering
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Qualitative methods in inverse scattering; weak scattering approximations (linear, needs a priori info); optimization techniques (nonlinear). Multiple scattering included in nonlinear approaches.
**Source:** AIM qualitative methods lecture; Aarhus inverse scattering; https://aimath.org/WWN/wavescattering/cakoni.pdf

### Q020: Time reversal wave information refocusing
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Time reversal experiments demonstrate refocusing of waves at source location. Multiple scattering enhances spatial resolution; refocusing better in heterogeneous media. Demonstrated for water waves and surface plasmons.
**Source:** arXiv 1211.0468; PRL 109 064501; arXiv 2111.09795; https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.0468

### Q021: Classical wave causality information transmission constraints finite speed
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Classical causality: equations weakly hyperbolic, normals to characteristics outside light-cone. Finite propagation speed is general result on hyperbolic equations.
**Source:** arXiv 2309.11703 causality dispersion relations; https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.11703

### Q022: Energy per bit classical wave transmission minimum Shannon Eb N0
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Minimum Eb/N0 = ln2 (-1.59 dB) at low spectral efficiency for AWGN channel. Optical energy per bit estimates ~1-4.4 fJ/bit.
**Source:** ICG Capacity Trends PDF; Stanford energy per bit; Berkeley low energy wireless; https://icg.isy.liu.se/en/phd-courses/optical_networking/Capacity_Trends.pdf

### Q023: Thermal noise classical wave information limit Johnson noise
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Johnson-Nyquist noise: <V²> = 4kTRΔf. NIST Johnson noise thermometry for Boltzmann constant determination; white noise below 100 MHz.
**Source:** NIST Jres 122.046; https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/jres/122/jres.122.046.pdf

### Q024: Wave dispersion information distortion transmission pulse spreading
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Chromatic dispersion causes pulse spreading; different wavelengths travel at different speeds. Third-order dispersion discussed; pulse shaping can reduce distortion. Optimum input Gaussian pulse width exists for max information capacity.
**Source:** PubMed 19862032 pulse distortion; UVA group velocity; PubMed 20111156 waveguide; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19862032

### Q025: Nonlinear classical wave information encoding solitons communication
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Solitons retain amplitude and shape while propagating; perfect for delivering information with reliability. Bright/dark solitons arise when nonlinearity and dispersion compensate. Fractional solitons in fluid environments studied.
**Source:** PMC12514162 fractional solitons; Springer Solitons book; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12514162

### Q026: Wave resonance information storage quality factor Q factor
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Q = resonance frequency / bandwidth; measures resonator "goodness". High Q = narrow bandwidth, high frequency selectivity. Quartz Q ~10⁴-10⁶, atomic clocks Q ~10⁸-10¹⁴.
**Source:** CCRMA Q factor; NIST Time and Frequency Q to Ra; https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/laplace/Quality_Factor_Q.html

### Q027: Standing wave information encoding nodes antinodes
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Standing wave = resonance mode from constructive interference of two waves traveling opposite directions. Nodes (zero amplitude) and antinodes (max amplitude) alternate. λ = 2L/n for string fixed at both ends.
**Source:** Physics Classroom nodes antinodes; OpenStax 16.6 Standing Waves; https://www.physicsclassroom.com/tutorial/vibrations-and-waves/standing-waves/nodes-and-anti-nodes

### Q028: Traveling wave versus standing wave information capacity
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Traveling waves not confined to given space; standing waves are resonance modes confined between boundaries. Traveling waves transport energy/information; standing waves store energy in localized patterns.
**Source:** Physics Classroom traveling vs standing waves; https://www.physicsclassroom.com/tutorial/vibrations-and-waves/standing-waves/traveling-waves-vs-standing-waves

### Q029: Classical wave polarization states information encoding
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Polarization is general feature of transverse waves in 3D. EM plane wave has two polarization states. Linear, circular, elliptical polarization. Polarized photon = qubit for QKD; little affected by atmospheric scattering.
**Source:** MIT 8.03SC Chapter 12 Polarization; MPL Lecture 10; AIP Encoding Information onto Light; https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-03sc-physics-iii-vibrations-and-waves-fall-2016/

### Q030: Wave orbital angular momentum information encoding classical OAM
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. OAM-based radio vortex wireless communications increase spectrum efficiency without extra time/frequency resources. ODM/HODM schemes proposed. THz OAM multiplexing demonstrated at 0.3 THz.
**Source:** IEEE OMDM; IEEE HODM; PubMed structured THz beams; https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8581019

## Batch Synthesis (Batch 1)

**Classical wave information theory:** All core results are ESTABLISHED. Information can be encoded in a finite wave's amplitude, frequency, phase, polarization, shape, spatial mode, wavefront, and temporal profile. Capacity is bounded by bandwidth × log(1+SNR) (Shannon). Finite propagation speed enforced by hyperbolic wave equations. Interference patterns and superposition enable multi-signal encoding and reconstruction. Time reversal can refocus waves at a source. All classical degree-of-freedom encoding techniques modulate a carrier — the carrier itself carries no information; the modulation does.

## Batch 2 Results — Q031-Q060: Quantum Wave Information & Encoding

### Q031: Qubit information capacity quantum wavefunction Hilbert space
**Result:** ESTABLISHED (bounded). Rational QM (Palmer, PNAS 2025) proposes finite qubit information capacity N_max ≈ 200–400 for current technologies, never exceeding 1,000, due to Hilbert space discretization. Established QM has N_max = ∞. Holevo bound: one qubit conveys at most 1 classical bit without prior entanglement; with shared entanglement (superdense coding), 2 classical bits per qubit.
**Source:** PNAS 2025 rational QM; arxiv 2510.02877; MIT Shor lectures.

### Q032: Quantum wave packet information encoding transmission matter wave
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Information transfer by quantum matter-wave modulation demonstrated: matter waves of electrons in a biprism interferometer used as carrier waves; data encoded via Wien filter introducing longitudinal shift between separated matter wave packets; receiver is delay line detector performing dynamic contrast analysis. Security via decoherence (eavesdropper destroys wave features).
**Source:** arxiv 2003.08555; IOPscience Data transmission by quantum matter wave modulation; APS March Meeting 2021; https://arxiv.org/html/2003.08555v1

### Q033: Quantum superposition information transmission wave interference
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Quantum interference = superposition of probability amplitudes (complex numbers). Classical waves divide amplitude (dividing energy); quantum interference divides wave function without dividing energy (photon cannot be divided). Each photon simultaneously exists in both arms of interferometer with finite probability amplitudes. Single photon interference 100% visibility vs classical 50% max.
**Source:** arxiv 2508.12940; Feynman Lectures Vol III Ch 3; https://arxiv.org/html/2508.12940v1

### Q034: Quantum wave interference information encoding probability amplitudes
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Photons encode information as photonic qubits where quantum state (polarization, momentum, energy) serves as carrier. Quantum interference rooted in superposition of probability amplitudes. Dirac: "Each photon interferes only with itself" — entities that interfere are probability amplitudes, not particles.
**Source:** arxiv 2508.12940 review of interference in QM.

### Q035: de Broglie matter wave information encoding transmission
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Any particle with energy and momentum is a de Broglie wave. Group velocity = particle velocity. Matter waves can be modulated for signal transmission; encoded binary on matter wave via Wien filter switching between high and low contrast.
**Source:** OpenStax University Physics Vol 3 Ch 6.5; arxiv 2003.08555 matter-wave modulation.

### Q036: Quantum wavefunction collapse information transfer measurement receiver
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Wavefunction collapse: system interacting with environment (measuring apparatus) transforms from superposition to definite state. No acausal behavior — collapse cannot transmit information faster than light. Measurement devices provide mechanism for collapse through particle creation/annihilation.
**Source:** Southampton collapse notes; arxiv 2405.05335; arxiv 2110.03721.

### Q037: Quantum entanglement wave information transmission no communication theorem
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. No-communication theorem: one observer cannot transmit information to another via entangled measurement regardless of spatial separation. Preserves causality/special relativity. Entanglement correlations alone cannot signal; requires classical channel bounded by light speed.
**Source:** arxiv quant-ph/9801014; Wikipedia No-communication theorem; https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9801014

### Q038: Quantum state tomography reconstruction partial measurement receiver
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. QST reconstructs density matrix from probabilistic measurements. Single-setting QST demonstrated using four-outcome measurements per qubit (ion trap, real-time 8-qubit reconstruction). Single-acquisition tomography of photonic qubits with structured media (metasurface diffraction patterns). Compressive QST reduces measurements from O(d²) to O(rd log d).
**Source:** PRX Quantum 2022 single-setting QST; arxiv 2607.03052 single-acquisition tomography; PMC5155294 compressive QST.

### Q039: Quantum wavefunction phase information encoding measurement
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Phase of wavefunction encodes physical information: interference, probability current (non-zero phase → non-zero current → state can change). Phase encodes momentum (wiggliness), energy, angle/angular momentum. Berry phase (geometric phase) used for fault-tolerant information encoding in superconducting qubits.
**Source:** Physics Stack Exchange phase intuition; arxiv 0711.0218 Berry phase qubit.

### Q040: Quantum matter wave information capacity single particle
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Single particle in quantum multiple-access channels: quantum setting strictly separates from classical. Single sender with shared phase reference: classical rate sum upper bounded by 1 bit; quantum setup experimentally observed 1.0152±0.0034 bits. Single particle coherently distributed across multiple spatial modes.
**Source:** Illinois Experts UIUC 2024 PRA 109 062420; https://experts.illinois.edu/en/publications/information-carried-by-a-single-particle-in-quantum-multiple-acce

### Q041: Schrodinger equation information propagation quantum wave packet
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Time-dependent Schrödinger equation governs wave function evolution. Wave packet travels at group velocity v_g = dω/dk = p/m (particle velocity). Wave packets disperse over time; width grows as σ(t) = [σ₀² + (ℏt/2mσ₀)²]^(1/2).
**Source:** UIUC Schrodinger Chapter 4; UMD quantum mechanics synopsis; https://www.physics.umd.edu/~jacobson/273b/Quantum_Mechanics.html

### Q042: Quantum wave packet dispersion information loss propagation
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Wave packet disperses as it propagates; rate governed by second derivative of ω(k) w.r.t. k. For electron wave packet at atomic scale (Δx~10⁻¹⁰ m), width doubles in ~10⁻¹⁶ s. Light waves: ω linear in k → no dispersion → pulses propagate without changing shape.
**Source:** Fitzpatrick evolution of wave packets; UVA group velocity lecture; https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/qmech/Quantum/node26.html

### Q043: Quantum state tomography partial measurement reconstruction density matrix
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. QST reconstructs density matrix. Compressive QST: O(rd log d) measurements suffice for rank-r state (vs O(d²) standard). Neural-network QST reconstructs states from limited measurements. Analytical optimization model improves reconstruction with restricted measurement sample size.
**Source:** PMC5155294 compressive QST; PRA 111 022601 analytical optimization; arxiv 2501.07404; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5155294

### Q044: Quantum wavefunction phase encoding observable
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Phase encodes momentum: plane wave ψ = Ae^(ikx), momentum operator p̂ = -iℏ d/dx → p̂ψ = ℏkψ. Phase derivative encodes momentum; phase curvature encodes energy. Time-dependent phase: e^(-iEt/ℏ) encodes energy.
**Source:** York University operators/expectation values; Brilliant wavefunctions measurement; https://www-users.york.ac.uk/~pjd113/markdown/2_2_OperatorsAndExpectationValues.html

### Q045: Quantum matter wave information capacity Holevo bound
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Holevo bound: classical capacities of quantum-classical channels corresponding to measurement of observables. Single photon: Holevo bound gives upper bound on channel capacity via spectral encoding. Continuous observables considered; explicitly solvable cases give entanglement-breaking channels with C_ea > C.
**Source:** arxiv 1103.2615 Holevo information capacity observable; PRA 88 022310; https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.2615

### Q046: Quantum wave packet dispersion spreading information
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Dispersive spreading of quantum wave packets unavoidable for normalizable wave packets (cannot suppress entirely without giving up normalization). Quantum chaos: exponential growth rate of wave packet spreading ≈ 2× classical Lyapunov exponent. Stroboscopic solitons can suppress spreading via periodic nonlinear kicking.
**Source:** PubMed 12006075 quantum dispersion; arxiv 1805.09870 overcoming dispersion; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12006075

### Q047: Quantum state tomography partial measurement reconstruction receiver
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Partial tomography poses challenges; analytical optimization model improves reconstruction over existing algorithms when restricting measurement sample size. Multiplicity of solutions in reconstruction problem depends on generated state and measurement model.
**Source:** PRA 111 022601 Prasad et al. 2025; https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevA.111.022601

### Q048: Quantum wavefunction phase encoding measurement observable
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. (Duplicate theme of Q039/Q044.) Phase encodes physical information: interference, observables. Wavefunction encodes all knowable information about quantum system. Position/momentum operators extract encoded information via expectation values.
**Source:** Dartmouth formalism QM; Illinois measurement QM PDF; https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/qm/qmhtml/S3.html

### Q049: Quantum matter wave information capacity single photon
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. (Duplicate theme of Q040/Q056.) Spectrally encoded single photon: Holevo bound upper bound on channel capacity. Analytic bounds derived; symmetric two-state encoding exact capacity calculated. In principle infinite information via continuous spatio-temporal degrees of freedom; realistic constraints reduce it.
**Source:** PRA 88 022310 Rohde et al. 2013; arxiv 1211.1427; https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevA.88.022310

### Q050: Quantum wave packet dispersion spreading information
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. (Duplicate theme of Q042/Q046.) Wave packet width grows linearly in time at rate proportional to d²ω/dk². Light pulses: ω linear in k → no dispersion → propagate without changing shape. Electron wave packets: ω quadratic in k → disperse; width doubles in ~10⁻¹⁶ s at atomic scale.
**Source:** Fitzpatrick wave dispersion; Schwartz lecture notes wavepackets dispersion; https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/355/Surveyhtml/node175.html

### Q051: Quantum state tomography partial measurement reconstruction

Semi-definite program places bounds on state expectation values when unique estimation not possible.
**Source:** NIST joint state-measurement tomography 2018; https://www.nist.gov/publications/joint-quantum-state-and-measurement-tomography-incomplete-measurements

**Result:** ESTABLISHED. (Duplicate theme of Q039/Q044/Q048.) Phase encodes physical information: interference, observables. Wavefunction encodes all knowable information about quantum system. Position/momentum operators extract encoded information via expectation values.

## Batch Synthesis (Batches 1–2)

**Classical waves:** All classical degree-of-freedom encoding techniques (AM/FM/PM/QAM/OAM/spatial modes/wavefront shaping) modulate a carrier — the carrier itself carries no information; the modulation does. Capacity bounded by Shannon limit. Finite propagation speed. Time reversal refocusing. All ESTABLISHED.

**Quantum waves:** Qubits/photons encode info in superposition states. Holevo bound limits classical info per qubit (1 bit without entanglement, 2 with superdense coding). No-communication theorem prevents FTL signaling. Wavefunction can be (partially) reconstructed via tomography/weak measurements. Wave packets disperse in dispersive media. Matter-wave modulation demonstrated for data transmission (Wien filter, electron interferometer). All largely ESTABLISHED.

**Key gap identified so far:** No existing work demonstrates transmitting previously unknown information not possessed by the receiver through a single finite wave where the wave's physical properties alone (not modulation of a carrier) enable reconstruction. All existing approaches modulate a carrier or use multiple particles/ensembles. This is the core XPOCK question.

## Batch 3 Results — Q061-Q090: Wave Degree-of-Freedom Encoding (phase/freq/amp/polarization)

### Q061: amplitude modulation information encoding classical wave carrier
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. AM: message signal impressed on carrier amplitude: s(t) = Ac[1 + m sin(ωm t)] sin(ωc t). Envelope carries message. Requires 1 + μm(t) ≥ 0 to avoid overmodulation. Carrier term bears no information — power wasted on carrier. Envelope follows message shape.
**Source:** GMU AM Modulation handout; CUHK Handout 4 AM; https://mason.gmu.edu/~rmorika2/AM_Modulation.htm

### Q062: frequency modulation information encoding wave capacity bandwidth
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. FM: information signal varies carrier frequency within small range. Bandwidth depends on both modulation index and modulating frequency (Carson's rule: BFM = 2(∆f + B)). FM more immune to noise than AM; larger bandwidth. FM radio band 88-108 MHz limits upper modulating frequency to 15 kHz.
**Source:** Navy NEETS FM module; ICG Communications lecture 14; https://man.fas.org/dod-101/navy/docs/es310/FM.htm

### Q063: phase modulation information encoding wave instantaneous phase
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. PM: angle of modulated wave used to carry information directly: θi(t) = 2πfct + kp m(t). Instantaneous frequency fi(t) = fc + kp ṁ(t). FM and PM are angle modulation twins — FM is PM of integral of message; PM is FM of derivative of message.
**Source:** CUHK Handout 7 Phase & Frequency Modulation; Wikipedia Phase Modulation; http://www.ee.cuhk.edu.hk/~wkma/eleg2310/handouts/7-FM.pdf

### Q064: polarization wave information encoding classical light degrees freedom
**Result:** ESTABLISHED. Polarization is parameter of light wave along with amplitude, wavelength, phase. Fully polarized lightwave SOP used as information-bearing unit in POLSK. Polarization very little affected by atmospheric scattering — good for free-space. Single photon always polarized; used as qubit for QKD.
