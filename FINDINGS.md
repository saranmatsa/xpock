# XPOCK Research Findings

## Topic
**Can information be transmitted through a single finite physical wave whose physical properties, structure, or interactions enable a receiver to reconstruct previously unknown information?**

## Methodology
- 1020 distinct research queries across 34 themes, searched in batches of 30 via `web_search`.
- Themes: classical waves, quantum waves, unconventional physical properties, information representation, physical interactions, reconstruction, existing research, unexplored combinations.
- Start from first principles; no assumption of a particular wave, mechanism, or architecture.
- Constraint: receiver must not already possess the information; not retrieval/selection/unlocking of pre-existing data.
- Laptop-only resources; no specialized hardware or paid infrastructure.
- Do not assume new physics is necessary; do not dismiss unconventional possibilities without investigation.
- Do not fabricate sources, research, or results.

## Evidence Classification
For each finding, flag as one of:
- **ESTABLISHED** — peer-reviewed / textbook consensus, reproducible.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — proposed but not conclusively demonstrated.
- **SPECULATION** — conjectural, no experimental basis.
